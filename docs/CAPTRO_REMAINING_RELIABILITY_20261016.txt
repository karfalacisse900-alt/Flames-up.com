2. OLDEST-40 STARVATION — SOURCE + LOCAL DATABASE VERIFIED
Root cause: unresolved purchases could repeatedly occupy the oldest-40 selection.
New service-only schedule stores due time, attempts, original purchase age, outcome,
lease token/expiry and attention state. Claims use SKIP LOCKED; four provider tasks
at once. Backoff 120 s to 6 h plus <=60 s jitter. Oldest due work wins: new arrivals
do not starve retries and repeatedly processing records do not monopolize batches.
Token checks reject stale completion. Terminal outcomes require matching database
evidence. Network/ambiguous/missing provider identity never means unpaid.
Missing-ID creation cannot blindly replay after 23 h or invalid/future timestamps:
provider idempotency retention is finite. Hold stays for explicit review instead.
Alerts after eight attempts/24 h; aggregate logs omit identities/payment details.
Restricted admin queue shows actionable ownership/age/next-attempt information.

3. PAID RECOVERY — IMPLEMENTED, DISABLED PENDING PROVIDER/STAGING ACCEPTANCE
A recovery-table insertion is NOT resolution. Buyer checkout/My Stuff/receipt say
payment received, access under review, do not pay again. Operator queue/detail
reads fresh provider state and records owner, decision, note, lease and attempts.
Write route requires payments:refund, rate limit and explicit rollout switch
CAPTRO_PAYMENT_RECOVERY_ACTIONS=approved (not configured by this work).
Fresh provider amount/currency/mode/identity/charge/refund checks precede resolution.
Fulfillment reacquires capacity transactionally and grants one entitlement. A
refund decision cannot later switch to fulfillment after a possibly sent refund.
Refund uses stable key, reconciles lost responses from provider list, and leaves
pending/ambiguous outcomes OPEN. A full verified refund records a final reference;
no entitlement or second inventory decrement. No destination-transfer reversal is
sent for deferred platform charges. Unsupported accounting/partial refunds require
manual review rather than guessing. Only supported released/unfulfilled cases use
this automated resolution path; already-transferred/disputed cases are rejected.
