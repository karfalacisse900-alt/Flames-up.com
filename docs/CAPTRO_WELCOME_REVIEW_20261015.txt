Resolution caveat (OPEN — final artwork approval pending)
Maximum artwork layout width: 480pt. At 3x and 1.03x zoom the required source is
ceil(480*3*1.03) x ceil(480*932/870*3*1.03) = 1484 x 1589 pixels.
At 440pt the required source width is 1360px, not merely 1320px.
The provided 870px-wide image is insufficient for genuine 3x detail. An exported
image with more pixels would not recover real detail. The image stays provisional
until a higher-resolution original is supplied or a new rendition is approved.
Physical-device sharpness has not been checked on this Windows host.
