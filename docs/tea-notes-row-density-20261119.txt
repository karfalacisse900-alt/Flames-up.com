Capture method
The real native root opens Tea with isolated transport fixtures. Avatar crops
come from the supplied reference and pass through the real image loader.
Fixtures require explicit review launch arguments and are compiled under DEBUG;
they are not published, production records, or release assets.
Each comparison job renders the previous row source, then restores the final
source and renders again on the same simulator UUID and default (.large) text
setting. App foreground screenshots are exported directly from XCTest.
Separate accessibility2 captures exercise larger text on both phone sizes.
The before/after compositor asserts matching simulator IDs. Screenshots are
only uniformly resized for side-by-side viewing, with labels outside the UI.
