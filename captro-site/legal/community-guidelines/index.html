Community Guidelines

Captro is for real people sharing real moments. These Guidelines explain what behavior and content is welcome, what is not allowed, and what may be removed or restricted.

Last updated: June 27, 2026 · Domain: https://captro.app/legal/community-guidelines

1. Be respectful

Treat other people with respect in posts, comments, chat, stories, profiles, and reports.

2. No harassment or hate

  • No bullying, threats, intimidation, or sustained abuse.
  • No hateful conduct, slurs, or dehumanizing language.
  • No violent threats or praise for violent acts.

3. No sexual exploitation

  • No explicit sexual content that violates Captro policy.
  • No sexual solicitation or exploitative content.
  • No content involving minors in sexual or exploitative contexts.

4. No scams, spam, or impersonation

  • No fake giveaways, phishing, financial scams, or fraudulent links.
  • No repeated spam posting, comment spam, or message spam.
  • No pretending to be another person, creator, or brand.

5. Respect privacy

  • Do not post private phone numbers, home addresses, IDs, passwords, or financial details.
  • Do not use place or location details to harass, threaten, or expose someone.

6. Respect ownership

Only share content you have the right to use. If you believe someone stole your work, report it with enough detail for review.

7. Enforcement

Content or accounts that break these Guidelines may be hidden, removed, restricted, suspended, or banned. Severe or repeated violations may lead to permanent removal.
