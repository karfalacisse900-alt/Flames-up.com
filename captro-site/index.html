A social app built for real people.

Captro combines photo sharing, stories, saves, comments, chat, blocking, reporting, moderation, and in-app account deletion. The app is designed for people age 16 and older.

Photos, stories, and discovery

Share photo posts, publish stories, and browse discover categories including photography, outdoors, art, nightlife, outfits, and events.

Social networking

Safer community tools

Report, block, and delete account inside the app. Captro uses moderation tooling and review workflows to help keep user-generated content safer and App Store-ready.

Safety first

Modern platform architecture

Supabase powers authentication and app data. Cloudflare Workers, Images, and Stream handle media, security, request validation, and content delivery.
