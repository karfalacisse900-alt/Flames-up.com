Your Privacy Choices

Captro gives users control over account data, content visibility, blocking, reporting, and account deletion. This page explains the main choices available inside the app.

Last updated: June 27, 2026 · Domain: https://captro.app/privacy-choices

1. Account and profile controls

  • You can edit your profile information inside the app.
  • You can change supported privacy settings from Settings.
  • You can remove or update profile photos and other public profile details.

2. Post and story controls

  • You can delete your own posts and stories.
  • You can decide whether to show optional place or city/country information where supported.
  • You can report harmful content and block abusive users.

3. Messaging and safety controls

  • You can block users so they cannot continue interacting with you where supported.
  • You can report users, posts, comments, stories, and messages for safety review.
  • You can manage notification permissions through iOS Settings and inside Captro where supported.

4. Delete account

Captro provides an in-app delete account flow. If you request deletion, your account may enter a deletion-pending state before permanent deletion or anonymization. During that window, your content is hidden from normal app surfaces.
